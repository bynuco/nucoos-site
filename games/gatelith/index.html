Questions and answers

Straight answers about Gatelith.

What is Gatelith?

Gatelith is a calm, satisfying sliding block puzzle set in a torch-lit temple of painted stone.

Is Gatelith free?

Yes. Gatelith is free to download and play. It shows ads. There are no in-app purchases: there is nothing to buy.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every level plays without an internet connection. Ads, and the optional extras you get by watching a short video, need a connection.

How many levels are there?

Gatelith has 500 levels.

Which phones and tablets can play it?

Android phones and tablets with Android 7.0 or later (64‑bit devices), on Google Play. The iPhone and iPad version is on its way to the App Store.

Which languages is it in?

English, Türkçe, Español, Português (Brasil) and Deutsch. The game follows your device language and uses English when yours is not on the list.

Is my progress saved? Do I need an account?

Your progress is saved on your device, and there is no account to create. If you sign in to Google Play Games, which is optional, a copy of your progress is also kept there, so it can follow you to another Android device.

Are there achievements and leaderboards?

Yes: 10 achievements and a Total Stars leaderboard on Google Play Games.

What data does the game collect?

Our privacy policy explains what the game and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have. Where the law requires it, the game asks before showing personalised ads, and you can change your answer in its Settings.

How do I report a bug or get in touch?

Email support@nucoos.com with your device model, the level number and what happened. A screenshot helps.
