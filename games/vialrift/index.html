Questions and answers

Straight answers about Vialrift.

What is Vialrift?

Vialrift is a turn-based capsule puzzle set in a hidden alchemist's crystal vault.

Is Vialrift free?

Yes. Vialrift is free to download and play. It shows ads. There are no in-app purchases: there is nothing to buy.

Can I play offline?

Yes. Every level plays without an internet connection. Ads, and the optional extras you get by watching a short video, need a connection.

How many levels are there?

Vialrift has 500 levels.

Which phones and tablets can play it?

It is coming to phones and tablets on Google Play and the App Store.

Which languages is it in?

English. The game follows your device language and uses English when yours is not on the list.

Is my progress saved? Do I need an account?

Your progress is saved on your device, and there is no account to create.

What data does the game collect?

Our privacy policy explains what the game and the services it uses collect, why, and the choices you have. Where the law requires it, the game asks before showing personalised ads, and you can change your answer in its Settings.

How do I report a bug or get in touch?

Email support@nucoos.com with your device model, the level number and what happened. A screenshot helps.
