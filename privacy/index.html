Privacy Policy — Nuco Games

Last updated: 2026-11-24

This policy covers every mobile game published by Nuco Games ("we", "our") on Google Play and the App Store. Nuco Games is an independent game developer based in Türkiye and is responsible for the processing described here. Contact: support@nucoos.com.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, precise location, contacts, photos or an account of our own. Game progress (levels, stars, settings) is stored on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it, unless you use Google Play Games or Game Center (below).

Google Play Games and Apple Game Center (optional)

On Android our games offer Google Play Games and on iOS Apple Game Center for achievements, a leaderboard and a cloud copy of your progress. Signing in is your choice: you can decline it and play the whole game without it. If you sign in, Google or Apple processes your gamer profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar), the achievements you unlock, your leaderboard score and the saved progress, under their own privacy policies (https://policies.google.com/privacy, https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We receive no email address or name from them. You can delete the game's Play Games data in the Google Play Games app (Settings > Delete Play Games account and data) or manage Game Center in your device settings.

Advertising

Our games show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iOS),
  • IP address and an approximate location (country or city level) derived from it, device model, operating system and
  • app version,
  • ad interactions (views, clicks) and diagnostic data such as app crashes and performance.

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings > Privacy Options. You can also reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Game statistics

Our games use Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which levels are too hard or too easy and whether players come back. It records game events (level started, won, lost, ad watched), an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version and an approximate country derived from the IP address. It is not used to identify you and is not used for advertising. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Emails you send us

If you write to us at support@nucoos.com, we receive your email address, your name if your email shows it, your message and anything you attach. We use them only to answer you and to fix the problem you report. Emails are kept in our mailbox (Google Gmail) and replies are sent through an email delivery service; an AI writing assistant may help us draft a reply, and a person approves every reply before it is sent. We keep your emails only as long as we need them to help you. To have them deleted, write to us with the subject "Data deletion": we delete them within 30 days and confirm by email.

Legal basis, retention and transfers

- Personalised ads in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland: your consent, asked in the game before any ad loads. Elsewhere, and for non-personalised ads and game statistics: our legitimate interest in funding a free game and improving it. - Emails you send us: our legitimate interest in answering you. - Retention: we store nothing on our own servers. Google keeps advertising and analytics data under its own retention rules (game statistics in Google Analytics for Firebase are kept for at most 14 months). - Google may process data outside your country, including in the United States, under the safeguards described in Google's privacy policy (such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses).

Children

Our games are made for teens and adults (each game's store page shows its age group) and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with Google (AdMob, Firebase and Gmail) and with the services that help us answer your emails, as described above.

Security and retention

We keep no personal data on our own servers. Data handled by AdMob and Firebase is kept according to Google's policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA) you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Contact us and we will help, or use the controls above.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
