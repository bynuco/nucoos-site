Privacy Policy — Nuco Games

Last updated: 2026-11-19

This policy covers every mobile game published by Nuco Games ("we", "our") on Google Play and the App Store.

What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos or any account. Our games have no sign-in. Game progress (levels, stars, settings) is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Advertising

Our games show ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID / Apple IDFA, only if you allow tracking on iOS),
  • IP address, device model, operating system and app version,
  • ad interactions (views, clicks).

How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before personalised ads, and you can change your choice at any time in the game's Settings > Privacy Options. You can also reset or limit the advertising ID in your device settings. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised ads.

Game statistics

Our games use Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which levels are too hard or too easy and whether players come back. It records game events (level started, won, lost, ad watched), an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system, app version and an approximate country derived from the IP address. It is not used to identify you and is not used for advertising. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Children

Our games are made for players aged 13 and over and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with Google (AdMob and Firebase) as described above.

Security and retention

We keep no personal data on our own servers. Data handled by AdMob and Firebase is kept according to Google's policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA) you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of your data. Contact us and we will help, or use the controls above.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
